Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About DK Electronics

Everything you need to know about our multi-brand products, warranty coverage, and showroom financing.

QAre all electronics sold at DK Electronics 100% genuine?

Yes. Every television, refrigerator, smartphone, laptop, and appliance is sourced through authorized brand channels with genuine manufacturer warranties and official serial tracking.

QHow does the 0% EMI financing process work?

We offer instant paperless financing in-store through Bajaj Finserv, HDB Financial, IDFC First, and credit card EMIs. Approvals take approximately 10 to 15 minutes with minimal documentation.

QCan I inspect and test the appliances before purchasing?

Absolutely. Our showroom features live demonstration zones for 4K OLED TVs, sound systems, smartphones, and laptops so you can test display quality, audio, and ergonomics in person.

QDo you provide express doorstep delivery and wall mounting?

Yes. We arrange safe transportation directly from our showroom to your doorstep, accompanied by certified technicians for professional wall mounting and electrical setup.

QHow can I inquire about unlisted models or specific prices?
